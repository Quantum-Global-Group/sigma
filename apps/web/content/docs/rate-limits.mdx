# Rate Limits

SIGMA enforces rate limits with a sliding window in Redis. Limits are applied per API key, not per IP.

## Per-plan budgets

| Plan | Calls / day | Burst (per minute) |
|------|-------------|--------------------|
| Free | 100 | 10 |
| Pro | 10,000 | 200 |
| Enterprise | Unlimited | 2,000 |

`Unlimited` is implemented as a very high ceiling — contact us if you expect more than 1M calls/day.

## Response headers

Every response includes:

```http
X-RateLimit-Limit: 10000
X-RateLimit-Remaining: 9847
X-RateLimit-Reset: 1735689600
```

`X-RateLimit-Reset` is a Unix timestamp at which the daily window rolls over.

## When you hit a limit

- HTTP `429` with `{ "detail": "Rate limit exceeded", "code": "RATE_LIMIT_EXCEEDED" }`.
- The burst limit refreshes after one minute; the daily limit resets at the start of your billing period.
- Cached signal responses (`cached: true`) do not count against either limit.

## Reducing your call volume

- Cache signals locally for the timeframe you requested (daily signals are stable for the trading day).
- Use [`POST /portfolio/rebalance`](/docs/endpoints) instead of looping per-ticker signals when you really want allocations.
- Schedule background jobs to back off on `429` with exponential delay (start at 2s).
